How data gets here

  1. Choose variables. Staff list Census variables in config/variables.csv in the Demographics repository.
  2. Download. The toolkit asks the Census API for each county and keeps only tracts and block groups inside the basin.
  3. Group. Detailed variables are added into TRPA categories, such as Age_Under_18 or BIPOC.
  4. Summarize. Values are added up to the Basin, each county's basin portion, North Lake and South Lake, and each state.
  5. Check. QAQC reports flag duplicates, gaps, and double counting before anything is published.
  6. Publish. A staff member reviews the files and loads them into the services this page reads.

Terms used on this page

American Community Survey (ACS) 5-year estimates
A Census Bureau survey that pools five years of responses. The 2023 estimate covers 2019 through 2023. Each estimate comes with a margin of error.
Decennial Census
The full count taken every 10 years (2000, 2010, 2020). It has no margin of error. Datasets include dec/sf1 and dec/sf3 (2000 and 2010) and dec/dhc (2020).
Margin of error (MOE)
The range around an ACS estimate that holds the true value 90 times out of 100. An estimate of 500 with a margin of error of 100 means 400 to 600.
Coefficient of variation (CV)
The margin of error divided by 1.645, divided by the estimate. Below 12 percent is reliable. Above 40 percent is unreliable.
Tract, block group, and block
Census geographies from largest to smallest. Tracts hold about 4,000 people. Block groups split tracts. Blocks are the smallest and appear only in the Decennial Census.
Census boundary year
Census redraws tracts and block groups every 10 years. ACS 2010 to 2019 uses 2010 boundaries. ACS 2020 and later uses 2020 boundaries.
TRPAID
The Census geography code plus the boundary year, stored as text. Tract 06017030201 on 2020 boundaries is TRPAID 060170302012020. It links every record to its shape.
Grouped variable
A TRPA category built by adding detailed Census variables. Its code starts with the grouping set name, such as "TRPA Grouped: B01001_003E, B01001_004E".
Summary geographies
Basin is the whole Lake Tahoe Basin. County rows cover only the basin part of each county. North Lake is Placer and Washoe. South Lake is El Dorado and Douglas. California and Nevada rows cover each state's part of the basin.
Medians
A median cannot be added across tracts. TRPA calculates basin medians from count bins, such as households by income range, instead.
